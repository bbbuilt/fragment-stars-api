# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help developers integrate Telegram Stars and Premium purchases safely.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Share enough technical context to solve the issue.
- Keep wallet seeds, Fragment cookies, private keys, and customer data out of public threads.
- Assume mistakes are fixable and focus on clear reproduction steps.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Posting another person's private data or credentials.
- Spam, scams, or attempts to sell unrelated services.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of wallet seeds or cookies.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close issues and comments that put users at risk or derail technical discussion.
